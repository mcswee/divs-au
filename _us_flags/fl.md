critique: |
  Florida's current flag combines a complicated state seal with a red saltire veiling Confederate symbolism. While the saltire is officially described as the Cross of Burgundy, the design does not resemble the traditional cross it claims to represent.
description: |
  My redesign replaces the current saltire with an actual Cross of Burgundy and replaces the seal with a simplified orange-sun element at the center.
symbolism:
  - title: "The Cross of Burgundy"
    text: |
      The Cross of Burgundy represents Florida's Spanish colonial history, replacing the current saltire with a more historically accurate version of the symbol.
  - title: "Orange"
    text: |
      The central element is colored orange and shows a cross-section of the fruit, representing Florida's citrus industry and its association with the state.
  - title: "The Sun"
    text: |
      The same element also represents Florida's sunshine and warm climate through its shape and rays.
