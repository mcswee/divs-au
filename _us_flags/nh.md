critique: |
  New Hampshire's current flag is another seal-on-a-bedsheet, cluttered further by the surrounding ring of stars and leaves.
description: |
  My design for New Hampshire uses the proposed state colors of red, orange, and yellow. I take inspiration from the Second New Hampshire Regiment, creating a red saltire and cross over a field split between yellow and orange. In the center, nine stars taken from the current flag sit at the center.
symbolism:
  - title: "The Second New Hampshire Regiment"
    text: |
      The saltire and cross motif is inspired by the design associated with the Second New Hampshire Regiment.
  - title: "The ninth state"
    text: |
      The nine stars represent New Hampshire as the ninth state to join the Union, retaining the ring arrangement of stars from the current flag, albeit smaller.
  - title: "State colors"
    text: |
      Red, orange, and yellow were proposed to be the official state colors of New Hampshire.
