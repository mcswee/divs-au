description: |
  Ohio possesses one of the great state flags, and I have no intention of changing its iconic swallowtail burgee shape or central Buckeye design. My only adjustment addresses the somewhat chaotic placement of the stars. While their current arrangement commemorates the original colonies and the states leading up to Ohio's admission to the Union, this is a flag for Ohio, not the thirteen colonies. I have neatened the layout by arranging all 17 stars in a unified circle surrounding the Buckeye.
