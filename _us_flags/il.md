critique: |
  Illinois's current flag is a classic seal-on-a-bedsheet design, but the bigger problem is that its symbolism could belong to any state. The eagle, shield and stars say "we're American" without saying anything about Illinois.
description: |
  The Illinois Centennial flag is the inspiration for my redesign, but I replace its 21 separate stars with a simpler arrangement of five stars. Four smaller blue five-pointed stars surround a larger six-pointed star in the center.
symbolism:
  - title: "The Centennial Flag"
    text: |
      The design draws directly from the Illinois Centennial flag, retaining its distinctive use of stars while simplifying the arrangement.
  - title: "The 21st State"
    text: |
      The four smaller stars represent the 20 states that preceded Illinois, with each point representing one of those states. The central six-pointed star represents Illinois as the 21st state.
  - title: "Fivefold Illinois"
    text: |
      The five stars also represent several groups of five associated with Illinois: the Indigenous nations of the Illinois Confederation, its five geographic regions, and the five Great Lakes. The central star's six points provide a visual link to Chicago's six-pointed flag star, while its position and contrasting colours can also represent Lake Michigan among the Great Lakes.
