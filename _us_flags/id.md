critique: |
  Idaho's current flag is another boring seal-on-a-bedsheet design. The detailed seal and name tag make for a cluttered flag with little identity.
description: |
  My redesign uses Idaho's unofficial state colors as the basis for a simpler design. A yellow field features red, white, and green chevrons pointing forward, with a six-pointed star made from six red diamonds at the fly.
symbolism:
  - title: "The Star Garnet"
    text: |
      The red gem-shaped star represents the star garnet, Idaho's state gem. Found only in Idaho and India, this rare gemstone is the source of the state's nickname, "the Gem State."
  - title: "Colors"
    text: |
      The yellow field, red and green chevrons, and red gem use the state's traditional colors. Green represents the landscape, yellow represents mining, and red represents the star garnet.
  - title: "Chevrons"
    text: |
      The chevrons represent progress, driving the state forward. They also reflect Idaho's mountains and the Snake River Valley.
