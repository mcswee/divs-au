critique: |
  Wisconsin's flag is currently a standard boring seal on a bedsheet. However, the crest contains a badger, which is a highly appropriate and established symbol for the state.

  My redesign moves away from the seal while keeping the black, white, and yellow colors. By evoking the stripes of a badger through a spiked pattern, the flag becomes fitting, unique, and visually distinctive.
symbolism:
  - title: "The badger"
    text: |
      The spiked pattern evokes the distinctive stripes of a badger, retaining one of Wisconsin's most established symbols without reproducing the animal itself.
  - title: "The colors"
    text: |
      The black, white, and yellow are retained from the existing flag, preserving a visual connection to Wisconsin's current design.
  - title: "Cheese"
    text: |
      The yellow spikes also represent Wisconsin's famous cheese industry.
