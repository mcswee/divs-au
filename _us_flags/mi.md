  - title: "Peninsulas"
    text: |
      The two stars represent Michigan's Upper and Lower Peninsulas, while their opposing orientation represents the Straits of Mackinac between them.
  - title: "The Great Lakes"
    text: |
      The five blue and white stripes represent the five Great Lakes that border Michigan.
  - title: "Naturally Michigan"
    text: |
      Green, blue, and white are frequently used in association with Michigan to represent its natural environment, lakes, and snow.
