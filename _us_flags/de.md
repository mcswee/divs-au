compare: true
critique: |
  Delaware's current flag is essentially a seal on a bedsheet, with the diamond providing its only real visual element. The crowded seal and accompanying date make for a cluttered design.
description: |
  My design replaces the state seal with a three-peaked zigzag band in the center of the flag. The existing buff and colonial blue palette is retained, while the text and seal are removed.
symbolism:
  - title: "De La Warr"
    text: |
      The zigzag band is taken from the coat of arms of Thomas West, 3rd Baron De La Warr, creating a direct visual link to Delaware's namesake and the West family lineage.
  - title: "The Three Counties"
    text: |
      The three peaks represent Delaware's three counties.
  - title: "The Landscape"
    text: |
      The jagged silhouette references the topography of the Delaware River and the Atlantic coastline. The position of the colors evokes the beaches and coast.
