  - title: "The Keystone State"
    text: |
      The three black keystones represent Pennsylvania's nickname, the Keystone State. They also represent the state's three founding counties.
  - title: "Penn's legacy"
    text: |
      The overall design evokes the heraldic design of William Penn's arms, with the three circles replaced by the keystones.
  - title: "Blue and gold"
    text: |
      The blue, white, and gold bands draw from the colors of Pennsylvania's iconic license plate, connecting the flag with a familiar symbol of modern Pennsylvania.
