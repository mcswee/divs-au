state_id: al
change_type: Recolor
compare: true
critique: |
  Vexillologically, the current red saltire on a white field is a sound design. However, it is also an obvious invocation of the Confederacy. The design's simplicity does not redeem its association with the Confederate battle flag.
description: |
  I retain the saltire but reverse its colours, making it white against a field divided into four alternating red and black sections.
symbolism:
  - title: "Peace"
    text: |
      The white saltire represents peace in a state with a history of racial division. Extending across the entire flag, it also symbolises the unity of Alabama and its people.
  - title: "The landscape and soil"
    text: |
      The red and black fields represent Alabama's varied soils: the red clay of the Piedmont region and the dark, fertile soils of the Black Belt.
  - title: "The Black Belt"
    text: |
      The black field also references the cultural Black Belt across central Alabama. Placing black along the hoist emphasises the region's importance to the state's identity and history.
